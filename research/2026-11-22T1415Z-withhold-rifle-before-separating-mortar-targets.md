# Withhold rifle-first against a separating mortar pair

Combat tested a trigger-only pulse while a real rifle and mortar were available. In simulated 1078677, an actual rifle hit advances 0.4 s and the saved mortar later hits both moving rivals. First-six outgoing gains 1,054.07 at zero incoming and placement gains one point. Under the same situation with slower separation, the native mortar lands first and the pulse loses 1,420.08 early outgoing. Placement still swings after these trades. Single-rival controls do not reproduce the earlier contact.

Combat voted to withhold before freeze. A later candidate needs a robust physical separation and line test plus a naturally active independent seed.
