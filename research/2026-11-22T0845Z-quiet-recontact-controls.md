# Quiet post-kill recontact controls

The following simulated cases use the queued `f99e200224f88cdd6c814e0728cdd145c31361d2` lineage. In 1073494 at 35.1, the mech has 10/12 rounds and presents a quiet scan after killing a rival. The new rival returns 1.3 seconds later when the torso scan brings it into view. The raw and raster paths were clear at the earlier observation. Forcing the refill gives up 291.6 first-six outgoing at equal zero incoming and loses one point. In 1074392 at 128.1, a previously unseen rival reappears after 0.8 seconds when the moving observer clears a wall. Forcing that refill gives up 291.6 first-six outgoing at equal 45 incoming and tied placement. Both observations lack the prior wall veto required by the current policy, and the exact confirmation build matches all native commands.

A different forced refill at 125.1 in gun-only 1074215 starts the same later reload earlier and advances an actual rifle hit on the same rival by one second. It gains 172.8 outgoing in six seconds at equal 45 incoming and two later placement points. In ordinary low-magazine 1074065 on `e63b373` at 121.8, holding reload until the actual rival appears advances the first landed rifle shot by two seconds but ties six-second damage and placement.

The independent route, weapon and evaluator controls support withholding an expanded quiet reload rule. Field of view and terrain can expose a rival on different timescales, and the gun-only cases show opposite outcomes. No prospective expansion was frozen.
