# Armor and beam timing under pressure

Two unfrozen experiments explored an independent combat family on the current recovery lineage. The local archive screen covered 60 campus recordings. Results are simulated.

Reactive Armor replaced Repair Nanites while injured, evading, and under observed rifle pressure. Of four relevant armor-and-nanites offers, only seed 1067239 met the rule. In its paired replay the first-six-second damage dealt tied and incoming fell by 18.8. A later lull let Nanites repair 57.6 HP early. The armor arm lost one place and one kill, and survived 0.8 seconds less. Observed crossfire did not establish that repair would remain unavailable.

A separate scratch deferred only new beam charge while rising along a route requiring jet movement. Twelve admissions appeared in seven recordings. Public seed 1067234 delayed a 327.6-damage beam by 0.4 seconds and fell from 15 to 9 placement points, with equal early incoming. Native seed 1067327 delayed a 403.2-damage beam by 0.4 seconds and lost one kill with the win intact. Full incoming increased by 3,115.8. Movement risk alone did not warrant delaying the first charge.

Both scratches were withheld before freezing or voting. A distinct follow-up could target a directly measured loss of shot clearance or a repair gap lasting beyond a real exchange.
