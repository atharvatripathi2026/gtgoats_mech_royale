# Test whether the drone targeting model changes crate outcomes

Local evaluator `0e84b2e1acf7d5ee872b60f5901a1e96253dbb0d` extends `8c44d0d` and changes drone target and hit eligibility only. The prior simulator processes drones exclusively against our bot. The alternate can select a nearby visible mech or retaliate against a recent attacker and can hit other live mechs. House pursuit can then change after actual damage. This is a model sensitivity test. The public rules still leave implementation and cooperation details uncertain, and no gameplay controller changed.

Across the same 16 fresh campus seeds in each model, `f090404` and `74d0c1f` matched all 78,085 commands and outcomes within their evaluator. The model lowered mean NPC inbound from 83.62 to 70.06 and moved the median first NPC contact among contacted matches from 24.1 to 16.75 seconds. Baseline placement also shifted between models. A separate 16 main and 16 standard public-upgrade pairs gained six points in main and tied under public upgrades. Another 16 same-lineage campus pairs from the carry study tied overall and reproduced earlier gear in a directed restart.

The alternate changed previous 1060415 from a public-upgrade three-point loss to a tie, with 0.2-second earlier equipment preserved and two drone hits on each arm of the pair. In 1061065, the same cube arrived 0.3 seconds and mortar 0.2 seconds sooner under all three evaluated rules at equal zero early trades. Later placement moved by +6 in main, zero in public-upgrade standard and +8 with alternate drone behavior. Most of the large incoming difference came during a much later encounter. The early acquisition mechanism replicated, and the later placement effect depended on the model. All results are simulated. [Prior crate retest](f090404-docker-recovery-and-independent-retest.md)
