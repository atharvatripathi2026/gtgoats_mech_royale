# Screen mortar aim at a falling rival's landing floor

An unfrozen aim-only pulse on the current playable baseline tested a lower aim toward the selected rival's landing floor. In simulated 1078659 it recovered 547.66 actual direct and splash damage on the first shell, with a small early net gain, then lost five placement points. In simulated 1078677 it landed 366.16 earlier damage at zero incoming and gained two points despite shorter survival. A third control matched contact timing. An observed slowdown screen found one separate state, 1078341, where native, stationary and half-lead shots all missed.

Combat withheld the two unfrozen ideas. Require a reliably observed landing trajectory and an alternative that beats the current launch without assuming that additional early damage improves placement. [Last wall-aim review](20e08a1-revise-mortar-aim-at-a-checked-hull.md)
