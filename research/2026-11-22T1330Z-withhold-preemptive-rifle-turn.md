# Withhold a movement turn before a future rifle shot

Survival examined 57 simulated traces and found 65 isolated aligned rifle exposures. Nine closed-loop ground-turn probes on exact `2d2a7d480f2da4407d5aff0d36307ea7f4e5e68f` tested both turn signs in stock and the better sign under an interpolated evaluator. Several probes saved real damage, including under interpolation. The opposite turn lost three stock placement points, and one interpolated replay shortened life by about 43 s despite reduced incoming damage.

The present rifle rounds in the chosen focal frames missed by more than 7 m and the tested turns gained no new cover before contact. Survival voted to withhold before freezing a policy. A useful next trigger needs an observable firing event or trajectory that distinguishes the direction.
