# A hidden contender can freeze the capture clock

Independent validation used actual Campus movement, a documented staged supply clock, and the old exact `2e5eec2c67f16174cd28ca5829a61da72e371a94` capture guard. A lone holder with 3.4 of four seconds banked completed the simulated capture at 99.0 s for three cubes and full repair. A physically moving rival entered the ring while still out of view. The guard held for two commands and released on the first frozen-progress observation. The original holder received no award and the rival became sole holder.

This staged physical control supports stopping on confirmed stalled progress and exposes the gap before the holder can observe the contest. Use real capture success and contest outcomes in any future candidate.
