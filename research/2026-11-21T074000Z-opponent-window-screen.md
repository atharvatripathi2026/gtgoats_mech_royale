# Screen brief retargeting opportunities

The opponent reviewer looked for a visible distracted opponent that could be finished while the native target had no legal shot. A retrospective simulated sample of 120 games and 217,417 recorded frames left 18 possible frames across two games. The alternate in 1040105 had about 1.01 times the gun’s observed hit damage while presenting front armor. In 1039202 the four-HP alternate was blocked during its away-facing interval. Twenty-five tested hull aim points per observation produced no projected legal hit in the useful interval. The prototype kept the original target in the directed test.

A follow-up matched six charged-laser frames in one archived game, 1037210. A 289.8-damage beam did arrive while the bot was dodging two other shooters. A projected early gun hit had no evidence of interrupting the charge and could have changed the active defensive sequence.

Validation ended both experiments before freezing gameplay. Before another retargeting proposal, require an actual same-target simulated hit during a legal firing window and identify which native shot or defensive action the switch would displace.
