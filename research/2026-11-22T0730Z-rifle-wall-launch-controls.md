# Rifle launch through nearby terrain · held for a prospective test

Combat screened 175 deduplicated simulated campus tapes and found 17 geometric warnings among 24,709 rifle-trigger observations. Several warnings accompanied actual damage. The directed simulations then instrumented the projectile’s real launch and first segment to separate genuinely blocked shots.

At 1073492, suppressing two confirmed blocked launches added two actual hits for 100.8 first-six outgoing at equal incoming, survival and placement. At 1073381, removing the blocked rifle trigger while preserving a new beam charge gained 75.6 early outgoing at zero incoming and a tied win. In 1073577, suppressing the blocked gun while the beam was already charging tied the early exchange but changed the result from 15 to 7 points. A one-command suppression sometimes just postponed the same blocked round.

Combat held the intervention as a directed diagnostic with no gameplay SHA or practice ballot. The next experiment should check the actual barrel path and first flight segment and require a confirmed blocked launch across plausible acceleration and shot spread. Use the already charged beam encounter as an exclusion control, then compare actual next-rifle hit timing on fresh seeds.
