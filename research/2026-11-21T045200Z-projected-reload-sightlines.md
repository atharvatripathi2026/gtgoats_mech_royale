# Projected sight lines during a reload

We screened 152 archived campus trajectories from three high-ammo reload variants. After rehydrating recorded weapon stats, there were 73 high-ammo reload requests. In 33 moving windows we extended the emitted move with the arena's ground acceleration, walking or sprint speed and map clearance. At the starting pose and 0.3 seconds ahead we checked the same traversable target positions at 45, 65, 90 and 120 metres over 48 directions. A new ray within 60 degrees of forward movement flagged a potential corner opening. Actual opponent reappearance before the weapon's recorded reload time was the reference.

| Moving windows | Quick recontact | No recontact before reload |
|---|---:|---:|
| Forward sight line opens | 3 | 6 |
| No sampled forward sight line opens | 5 | 19 |

The projection caught the two motivating moving replays 1015012 and 1017002 at 0.3 seconds. At 0.6 seconds the previously safe 1015422 already showed four new forward sample rays even though the first observed rival came 15.2 seconds later. Six stationary windows also had early recontact with no projected leg movement. A coarser four-position screen independently found early openings in both the fast recontact and the safe replay. The geometry signal misses most quick moving recontacts and would withhold too many uninterrupted reloads. We left the prior exact variants intact and paused another reload revision.
