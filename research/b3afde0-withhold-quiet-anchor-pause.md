# Pause after a repeated shelter reversal

Exact `b3afde0c504a19df3d517a8b6851e949e65a0c84` on `5f0ace9121f41f8ff702c7e94f5494649c4671da` lets the grounded mech hold briefly after it twice leaves its nearby zone anchor for distant shelter, with both decisions starting within 1.5 m of one another. The pause requires current and forecast circle margin, quiet sensors and no observed mech or NPC within 65 m. It runs once per shrink phase and interrupts when the native planner sees loot or an immediate threat. It changes motion and can change scan aim or the loadout's steady signal.

All outcomes are simulated. Four directed stock campus squad16 replays on seeds 1073039, 1073040, 1072942 and 1072947 changed commands at 39.7, 135.4, 249.7 and 114.4 s. Placement and the first six seconds of damage tied in each. Survival differences were −62.05, +0.30, −0.60 and −0.55 s. Over the first seven observations the held mech traveled 0.02–0.05 m instead of 2.13–2.37 m. After release the loops resumed. In the first two replays one hop was removed over six seconds, while mode switches fell 17→13 and rose 12→13, respectively.

The second seed has more shelter/zone switches after the pause. Eight fresh pairs (1073090–1073097) produced six command ties, one changed-command placement tie and one loss. Seed 1073090 lost one point and 32.2 s of life at unchanged first-six damage. Seed 1073096 fired one extra 81-damage rear gun hit on rival #0 by 120.9 s, then later took 67.5 more total damage at tied placement and survival.

The exact build passed 23 unit tests and all 300 timed networkless kit observations. Three GPT-6.2 Sol reviewers voted **revise**. Survival found the pause could delay the same routing loop and cut life by 62.05 s. Combat found no immediate payoff in the four directed stock games and a fresh loss under faster closing. Validation found the later alternate gains did not reliably track fewer switches or repeat on fresh seeds. Withhold this commit. Before revisiting, require measurable progress after release or a verified route that remains stable across the shelter threshold.
