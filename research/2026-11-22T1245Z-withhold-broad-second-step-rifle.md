# Measure second-step rifle timing before changing fire policy

Combat ran three one-command simulated interventions on stock against the latest two solo-policy lineage. In 1077430 a held trigger advanced an actual 48.6 hit by 0.1 s at equal early damage and placement. In 1077191 the pulse launched one 50 ms physics step earlier and added a 72.9 hit on rival 10. The apparent concurrent hit on rival 3 was a previous round already in flight. In 1077429 the same rival 12 kill time was followed by an extra 94.5 hit on rival 7. The last two cases add outgoing at equal early incoming and tie placement. An interpolated 1077191 control reaches that observation with an empty gun and reload in progress, leaving the pulse inactive.

Combat withheld the broad timing change before freezing a policy. The next test needs a ready rifle on the second step, a clear projected muzzle and target corridor, and a control where a different rival could intercept the earlier round.
