# Lightning actor order and laser interruption

The baseline and candidate are `02e756efb79ff95d7be6d82e44e98f51fc1bdba6` and `a11e87a5dd0c484f7ffd1537f4226b15159f9e34`. The following results come from local simulations with distinct assumptions about referee behavior.

**Actor order.** Validation used its public-upgrade evaluator `8c44d0daf18c5ed648225963d9f93534ea09b01f` and the all-mechs-move-first variant `3c1bb8f69c8d40d9afbb13fffe750b86f72ff4d5`. The candidate lost nine total placement points under the original order and tied under the phased order on the same 16 fresh campus seeds 1061400–15. The only loss common to both variants, 1061414, shrank from eight points to one. Its first added 76.56-damage arc hit, and actual damage through six seconds was equal in both builds. Seed 1061401 gained a point with either order after a real added hit. All 64 phased four-mech placements and recorded endpoints tied on 1061420–27. Both campus panels retained complete trajectories. The phased four-mech panel retained per-game ledgers. All 37 evaluator tests passed.

Moving the target first also turned the prior offset-muzzle `e5c037e` dry discharge in directed seed 1060940 into a 72-damage hit. The more conservative queued policy still finished a point ahead of that offset-muzzle build on the same seed. Combat’s separate archive check found 24 directed fringe pulses that hit. Twenty-two hit the selected target and two hit another mech. None of the 24 needed the target to move into physical range or cone. Adding a stricter margin would have suppressed legitimate hits.

**Laser interruption.** The separate `731e69c41ae49a855f5ca6df52afca2907b559f9` evaluator models the public lightning effect on equipped lasers. Direct checks verified charge reset, suppression before expiry and resumed charge at expiry. The stock and short-aware evaluators both produced +5 total across 16 fresh campus seeds 1061440–55, with +5 on 1061444 and 15 placement ties. Both preserved +2 on directed 1061242 and +11 on 1061307. Directed 1061242 did change some damage and survival totals even with identical placing. Closed-loop traces were retained locally. The public guide documents the lightning effect. [Starter kit and protocol](https://www.joinbuilders.org/buildathon/tracks/builders)

Leave the already queued `a11e87a` choice in place. Use an actual same-target early damage advantage and the subsequent native shot opportunity to judge marginal fringes in future variants. Both evaluator variants remain local research tools.
